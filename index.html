About the course

This course introduces the principles and engineering applications of modern Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML). You will learn core machine learning methods, including regression, classification, clustering, dimensionality reduction, and anomaly detection, along with neural networks and deep learning architectures.

It also introduces modern generative AI: transformers and large language models, plus the high-level ideas behind VAEs, GANs, and diffusion models. The emphasis is on mathematical intuition, practical implementation with modern AI frameworks, engineering data pipelines, and real-world applications. Ethical and responsible AI (bias, fairness, privacy, and security) is discussed throughout.

You finish with a semester project: designing and evaluating an AI-based system for an engineering application.

Before you enroll

  • Python: proficiency in Python, including programming environments and basic libraries.
  • Calculus: MATH 151 and MATH 152.
  • No prior AI needed: the linear algebra, probability, and statistics you need are taught in Weeks 2–4.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain the core principles of AI and machine learning: key algorithms, neural networks, and ethical questions such as bias, fairness, privacy, and security.

  • Apply supervised learning (regression, classification, and ensemble methods) to analyze and solve engineering problems.

  • Apply unsupervised learning (clustering, dimensionality reduction, and anomaly detection) to explore and interpret complex datasets.

  • Design and train neural networks with modern AI frameworks, and understand training dynamics, evaluation, and optimization.

  • Explain generative AI and transformer models: attention, large language models, and the ideas behind VAEs, GANs, and diffusion models.

  • Design, build, and present an AI-based engineering project, with performance evaluation and responsible AI built in.

The semester project

In teams, you design, build, and evaluate an AI system for an engineering application. Some examples:

  • Predictive maintenance system
  • Intelligent sensor analytics
  • Computer vision application
  • AI-driven signal processing
  • LLM-powered engineering assistant
  • AI anomaly detection for networks

What you deliver

  1. ProposalWeek 8
  2. ImplementationAfter the proposal
  3. Technical reportWith the final project
  4. Presentation & demoWeeks 15–16

How you are graded

  • Exams · 45%Four exams on the mathematical foundations, core machine learning, neural networks, generative models, and advanced AI techniques.
  • Project · 35%A semester-long project: design, implementation, evaluation, and a final presentation of results.
  • Attendance · 10%Lectures, in-class demonstrations, and discussions.
  • Participation & discussion · 10%Contributing to discussions, asking questions, and learning together.

Letter grades: A ≥ 90% · B 80–89.99% · C 70–79.99% · D 60–69.99% · F < 60%.
